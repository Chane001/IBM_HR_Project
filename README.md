# IBM_HR_Project
HR Attrition analysis using Excel and Tableau

## Business Problem
Why are employees leaving the company, and what factors contribute to attrition?

## Dataset Overview
- Total Employees: 1,470  
- Employees Leaving: 237  
- Attrition Rate: **16.12%**

  ## Business Questions
- Which departments have the highest attrition?
- Does overtime increase the probability of leaving?
- Does salary level influence attrition?
- Does job satisfaction correlate with attrition?
- Does experience at the company impact turnover?
- Do working conditions influence attrition?

## Dashboard Overview
link: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/chane.claquin/viz/IBM_HR1_17743394606000/Dashboard3

### Dashboard 1 – Employee Attrition Overview & Workforce Analysis?
Provides a high-level view of attrition and identifies the most affected employee groups.

### Dashboard 2 – What factors influence employee attrition?
Analyzes key drivers of attrition, including overtime, compensation, and job satisfaction.

### Dashboard 3 – Attrition Risk Analysis: linking employee profiles to key drivers
Combines employee demographics and key drivers to identify the root causes of attrition within high-risk groups.

## Key Insights
- Early-career employees (≤2 years) have the highest attrition rate (**29.82%**).
- Sales and customer-facing roles show significantly higher turnover.
- Overtime is a major driver, with attrition reaching **30.53%**.
- Lower-income employees are more likely to leave (**28.61%**).
- Low job involvement and poor work-life balance strongly increase attrition.

## Combined Insights

- High attrition groups (early-career employees and sales roles) are also exposed to key risk factors such as overtime, lower income, and lower job satisfaction.
- This suggests that attrition is driven by a combination of workload pressure, compensation, and employee experience.

## Recommendations
- Reduce overtime and improve workload distribution  
- Improve compensation for lower-income employees  
- Increase employee engagement and job involvement  
- Improve work-life balance policies  
- Enhance workplace environment and satisfaction
